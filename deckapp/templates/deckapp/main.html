FAQ

Is this truly random?
Yes. We use a secure shuffling method and cryptographic hashing to ensure randomness.
Can someone else get the same shuffle as me?
Theoretically yes, but it’s astronomically unlikely. That’s the beauty of 52! permutations.
How do you track unique shuffles?
Each shuffle is hashed using SHA-256 and compared to a database of seen hashes.
